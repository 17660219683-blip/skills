# 融合提示词模板参考

常见产品-场景组合的提示词结构模板。

## 饮品瓶/罐 + 户外自然场景

```
A [product description] placed on [surface] in [scene], [lighting description], [perspective], [atmosphere details], photorealistic, product photography, professional lighting, 8k, highly detailed
```

例：`A glass kombucha bottle with amber liquid and minimal label placed on a mossy rock by a forest stream, soft dappled sunlight filtering through canopy leaves, warm green and brown color palette, shallow depth of field with stream bokeh in background, photorealistic, product photography, 8k`

## 电子产品 + 桌面/室内场景

```
A [product description] resting on [surface] in [indoor scene], [lighting description], [perspective], clean minimal composition, [atmosphere], product photography, studio lighting, ultra detailed, 8k
```

例：`A sleek silver laptop with glowing logo resting on a wooden desk in a modern minimal home office, soft window light from the right, warm neutral tones, clean composition with a coffee cup and notebook nearby (soft focus), product photography, professional studio lighting, ultra detailed, 8k`

## 美妆/护肤品 + 浴室/梳妆台

```
A [product description] placed on [surface] in [scene], [lighting], [perspective], [color palette], soft elegant atmosphere, product photography, luxury aesthetic, 8k
```

例：`A white ceramic skincare jar with gold accents placed on a marble vanity surface in a bright minimal bathroom, natural daylight from above, soft shadows, clean reflection on marble surface, pastel and white color palette, soft elegant atmosphere, luxury product photography, 8k`

## 鞋服/配饰 + 城市/街景

```
A pair of [product description] placed on [surface] in [urban scene], [lighting], [perspective], street photography style, [atmosphere], cinematic, 8k
```

例：`A pair of white minimalist sneakers placed on a weathered concrete step in a narrow alley with warm sunset light, low angle perspective highlighting the shoe silhouette against the cobblestone texture, cinematic street photography style, warm orange and gray tones, 8k`

## 食品/餐具 + 餐桌/野餐场景

```
[Product description] arranged on [surface] in [scene], [lighting], [perspective], [color palette], food photography style, appetizing lighting, shallow depth of field, 8k
```

例：`A warm croissant in a small woven basket placed on a picnic blanket in a sunny park, soft golden hour sunlight, overhead flat lay perspective, fresh green grass and white daisies in background, warm beige and green palette, food photography style, appetizing lighting, 8k`

## 参考原则

1. **场景先行**：开头先描述场景定位，再引入产品
2. **光照具体化**：说明光源类型、方向、色温，而非仅写"good lighting"
3. **位置明确**：产品放在什么上面/里面/旁边，写清楚
4. **质感词**：用材质词（glossy/matte/transparent/rustic/weathered 等）传递触感
5. **避免歧义**：不使用 ambiguous 的形容词，用具体视觉描述替代
6. **风格标识**：末尾加上风格标签（photorealism / cinematic / minimal / luxury 等）
7. **质量后缀**：根据目标工具选择合适的 quality keywords
8. **保持一致**：用一个语言（英文）完成整段 prompt，不要中英混写
